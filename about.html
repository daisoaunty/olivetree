Our story

Founded in 1998, run with the same values.

Olive Tree Baby & Kids Clinic has grown into a reputable name in Singapore's paediatric landscape, serving three generations of the same families today — the same unhurried, collaborative approach we started with.

Our Doctors

Two familiar faces, not a rotating roster

This practice is run primarily by Dr Tan Siew Pin as the anchor paediatrician. On Tuesdays, Dr Mary Lim Li Ann is the in-house paediatrician and fills in for Dr Tan.

Dr Tan Siew Pin

Founder & Anchor Paediatrician

A mother of four and a strong advocate of breastfeeding, Dr Tan is known for her collaborative approach — engaging both parents in a shared care plan for their child. She takes the time to give thorough explanations, giving parents the knowledge and reassurance to navigate their child's health and developmental journey. She also works closely with mothers to address breastfeeding challenges.

  • MBBS (NUS YLL, Singapore), 1985
  • MMed, FAMS (Paediatrics), 1989
  • Specialist Paediatrician, NUH, 1989–1998

Dr Lim Li Ann Mary

In-house Paediatrician (Tuesdays)

Dr Lim brings over three decades of paediatric medicine experience to the practice, providing continuity of care on Tuesdays alongside Dr Tan.

  • Registration No. M05514Z
  • MBBS (NUS YLL, Singapore), 1990
  • MMed, FAMS (Paediatrics), 1994
  • Specialist Paediatrician, NUH, 1999
