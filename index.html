Our philosophy

“We are committed to the holistic wellbeing of your child, in a clinic designed to be a nurturing, family-oriented environment.”
Dr Tan Siew Pin, Founder

Founded in April 1998, Olive Tree Baby & Kids Clinic has become a reputable name in Singapore's paediatric landscape for our committment to providing the highest quality healthcare for your child. We hope every consultation leaves you and your child feeling genuinely heard in a supportive and stress-free environment.

Services provided

Care that grows with your child

From newborn checks to school-age assessments, delivered by the same familiar faces each visit.

Well Baby & Child Checks

Routine growth and development monitoring, including newborn assessments for hospital-born babies.

Vaccination & Immunisation

Full childhood vaccination schedules, with individual vaccine consultations available.

Developmental Assessments

Tracking milestones and flagging concerns early, with referral pathways where needed.

Breastfeeding Advisory

Hands-on support for common breastfeeding challenges, working with mothers toward a tailored plan.

Acute & Chronic Illness

Fever, coughs, colds and diarrhoea, asthma, eczema, allergic rhinitis and bed-wetting.

Health Assessments

School entry, sports and pre-camp health checks, plus inpatient care at Singapore's major private hospitals.
