What we offer

Care for every stage of childhood.

From a newborn's first check-up to school-entry health assessments, our services are built around continuity — the same doctors, the same clinic, as your child grows.

Well Baby & Child Checks

Routine growth and development monitoring at every age and stage, including newborn assessments for hospital-born babies.

Vaccination & Immunisation

Full childhood vaccination schedules following Singapore's national programme, with individual vaccine consultations also available.

Developmental Assessments

Tracking speech, motor, and social milestones, with early flagging of concerns and referral pathways where needed.

Breastfeeding Advisory

Hands-on, judgement-free support for common breastfeeding challenges, working with mothers toward a plan that fits their family.

Management of Acute Illnesses

  • Fever
  • Coughs
  • Colds
  • Diarrhoea

Management of Chronic Illnesses

  • Asthma
  • Eczema
  • Allergic rhinitis
  • Bed wetting

Health Assessments

  • School entry
  • Sports
  • Pre-camp

Hospitalised Patient Care

Attending to admitted patients across Singapore's major private hospitals:

  • Mount Elizabeth
  • Gleneagles
  • Thomson Medical Centre
  • Mount Alvernia
  • East Shore

Adoption & Abortion Counselling

Pre-adoption and pre-abortion counselling offered pro bono, alongside formal adoption and abortion assessments.
